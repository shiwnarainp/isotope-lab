Isotope Lab

Discovering isotopes and calculating average atomic mass, from carbon-12 to the 35.45 on the periodic table.

Learning goals

Essential question: How do isotopes of the same element differ, and how does their relative abundance determine an element’s average atomic mass?

Today I will investigate isotopes and calculate the weighted average atomic mass of elements. So I can explain how different neutron numbers produce isotopes and how abundance shapes average atomic mass.

I am successful when I can:
  1. Define an isotope.
  2. Find protons, neutrons, and electrons in neutral isotopes.
  3. Explain why isotopes of an element have different mass numbers.
  4. Distinguish atomic number, mass number, and average atomic mass.
  5. Interpret isotope notation.
  6. Explain percent abundance.
  7. Convert percent abundance to decimal form.
  8. Calculate average atomic mass as a weighted average.
  9. Predict which isotope contributes most.
  10. Interpret average atomic mass on the periodic table.
